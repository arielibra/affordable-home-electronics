# Affordable Home Electronics — design brief

## Design read
For a homeowner or shop owner in Queens who decided tonight that they want cameras,
and for a contractor across the country who needs a genuine Hikvision NVR with a real
USA warranty. The register is calm competence: this is the counter of a place that has
been selling and installing this gear since 2003 and knows the part number by heart.

## Concept spine
**The monitor wall.** The page is built like a well run control room: a disciplined grid
of framed views, precise small labels, generous quiet space between them, and one live
red indicator that says something is being recorded. Every section is another feed on
the wall.

## Delivery tier
`editorial`. The user picked Non-animated at intake. Craft floor held with bespoke
generated assets, a real typographic system, and motivated micro motion (framed feed
reveals, magnetic CTA physics, sequential grid staggers). No scroll journey.

## Animation mode
Animation mode: non-animated — user picked Non-animated at intake

## Locked palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#f2f3f5` |
| Surface | `#ffffff` |
| Ink | `#13171d` |
| Muted | `#606a78` |
| Line | `#d9dce1` |
| Accent (single, page wide) | `#d2273f` |

Defense: security is sold on trust, not menace, so this site works in daylight instead
of the default dark plus neon cyan surveillance cliche. One saturated red carries the
only idea that matters (this is live, this is recording) and it is the sole chromatic
event on an otherwise achromatic instrument palette.

## Locked type
`Geist` for display and UI, `Geist Mono` for part numbers, prices, spec labels and the
small monitor wall captions. Display base `text-4xl md:text-6xl tracking-tighter
leading-none`. No serif anywhere.

## Section plan (home)
1. Hero, asymmetric split: left stack, right framed feed
2. Shop categories, horizontal scroll rail
3. What we actually do, 2 col zigzag
4. Local installation NY, full bleed service band with form
5. Featured gear, product grid on live store data
6. Why buy from us, asymmetric bento
7. Brands carried, logo wall
8. What customers say, quote row
9. Contact, split panel into footer

Eyebrow budget: 3 (ceil 9/3). Layout families: 9 distinct, no consecutive repeats.

## Asset plan
Hero visual (2 candidates), 3 section plates, 6 category plates, an 8 glyph custom icon
set in one stroke style, wordmark plus monogram plus full head kit, OG card, 404 art,
empty cart artwork.

## CTA inventory (bespoke chrome, no shared button class)
- `Shop cameras` (hero primary): solid ink slab, red rule that draws left to right on
  hover, `:active` scale 0.98
- `Call 718.275.9343` (hero secondary): ghost with hairline border, mono numerals, hover
  fills paper to surface
- `Get a quote` (installation band): red field, white label, arrow steps right on hover
- `Add to cart` (product card and detail page): full width hairline box, label swaps to a
  mono confirmation state
- `Checkout` (cart): ink slab carrying a single red record indicator that lights on hover
- `Send message` (contact form): outlined, corner brackets that close on focus within
